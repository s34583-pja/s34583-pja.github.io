Typography & Form

Typography is the silent voice of design. It is the architectural discipline of arranging type to make written language legible, readable, and appealing when displayed.

This project explores the fundamental structures of letterforms, the anatomy of glyphs, and the profound impact that typographic choices have on brand perception and visual hierarchy.
