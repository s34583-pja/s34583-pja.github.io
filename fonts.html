Google Fonts

A curated selection of typographic styles, demonstrating variable characteristics and visual contrast.

Ballet

An elegant, variable script font designed by Maximiliano Sproviero. It offers extreme contrast and graceful curves, perfect for sophisticated visual identities.

Urbanist

A low-contrast, geometric sans-serif inspired by Modernist typography. Its pure forms and balanced spacing make it highly legible across digital interfaces.

Cormorant

A classic serif typeface inspired by the 16th-century legacy of Claude Garamont. It features elegant curves and sharp counters for a refined editorial aesthetic.

Space Mono

An original fixed-width type family tailored for editorial use. It combines geometric structure with industrial details, adding a tech-driven edge.
